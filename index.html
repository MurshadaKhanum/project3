Delicious Chicken Pasta

Chicken pasta is a delicious and easy meal that you can prepare at home.

Preparation Time: 15 Minutes | Cooking Time: 20 Minutes | Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 150g chicken
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 2 tablespoons tomato sauce
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • Cheese (optional)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta in water until soft.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and cook the chicken.
  3. Add chopped onion and cook until golden.
  4. Add tomato sauce, salt and black pepper.
  5. Pour in the milk and mix well.
  6. Add the boiled pasta and stir everything together.
  7. Garnish with cheese and serve hot.

Enjoy Your Meal!

Serve your delicious chicken pasta while it is hot.
